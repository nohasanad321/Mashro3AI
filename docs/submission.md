# Mashro3 AI — local retrieval prototype

A bilingual order desk for small home businesses. Customer messages are matched against local catalogue aliases; explicit details are extracted with rules. Owners review and confirm persistent orders.

Business knowledge questions retrieve cited excerpts from local policy documents. No language model, embedding model, cloud service or generated answer is used. This implementation is retrieval-only, not generative RAG. Unrecognized phrasing, unknown products, negations and repeated mentions require manual review. No accuracy claims are made.

Demo: English or Arabic sample → extract → review missing address → save → confirm → reload. Show the knowledge search and its source citations. Use synthetic data.

Public repository: https://github.com/nohasanad321/Mashro3AI
